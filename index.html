About Me

Hey there — I'm Mohamadi, a software engineer from Egypt, specifically a backend engineer, but I think you can already tell from the simplistic UI of this website :). I’ve been building software for a few years now, helping turn small ideas into real products that serve actual humans, which still feels kind of magical. I find my biggest interest in designing databases, writing efficient SQL queries, and crafting solid APIs. When I’m not coding, I’m either playing video games, tinkering with personal projects, or pretending I don’t have a dozen tabs open reading about some new tech I’ll probably try once and never touch again. If you're building something interesting, and you think I could help, feel free to reach out. I’m always up for a good conversation — and maybe a new challenge.
